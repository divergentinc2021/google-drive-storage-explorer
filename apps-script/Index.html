Copying to the NAS is a different job, and there is a tool for it. This dashboard tells you what to move; it cannot reach the NAS at all — Apps Script runs on Google's servers and has no route to a LAN address. Storage Mapper is the desktop app that does the move: it finds what is already on the QNAP under a different name, refuses to copy Google-native stubs, and never deletes anything.
